Student Loan Manager for CDSGA

C.A.R.E is the official mobile app for students of Colegio de San Gabriel Arcangel. Apply for loans, track your balance, make payments through GCash, Maya, or bank transfer — all in one app.

Features

📝 Apply Online

Submit loan applications in under 2 minutes.

💰 Multiple Payment Options

Pay via GCash, Maya, credit/debit card, bank transfer, or cash.

📊 Track Your Balance

View your remaining balance, amortization schedule, and payment history.

📄 Upload Documents

Submit school ID, government ID, and enrollment proof securely.

🔒 Verified Accounts

All student accounts are verified by school managers.

📱 Access Anywhere

Check your loan status anytime, anywhere on your phone.

How to Install

  1. Tap the Download the App button above.
  2. Open the downloaded app.apk file.
  3. If Android asks, enable "Install from unknown sources" for your browser.
  4. Install the app and open it.
  5. Log in with your student number.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app free?

Yes. The app is free for all CDSGA students.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and protected by Row Level Security on our backend. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.

What if I forget my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen, or contact your school manager.

Why does Android warn about installing from unknown sources?

Because the app is not distributed through Google Play. This is normal for school or thesis apps. Tap "Install anyway" to continue.
